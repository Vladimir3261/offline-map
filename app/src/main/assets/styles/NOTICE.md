# RouteOdo map styles

RouteOdo day and night styles adapt OpenFreeMap Liberty label definitions, with a new road palette, simplified layers, and reduced clutter. Application chevrons and maneuver/control icons are original bundled Android assets/code.

- OpenFreeMap styles: https://github.com/hyperknot/openfreemap-styles
- OSM Liberty: https://github.com/maputnik/osm-liberty
- OpenMapTiles schema/design: https://openmaptiles.org/ (CC BY 4.0)
- OpenStreetMap data: https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright

Upstream license notices are bundled alongside this file. MapLibre’s attribution control preserves the OpenStreetMap/OpenMapTiles credits supplied by the vector source. No proprietary Waze/Google assets are included.
